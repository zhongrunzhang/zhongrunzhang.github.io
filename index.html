Runzhang Zhong

Postdoctoral Fellow in mathematics at the University of Missouri

I work on partial differential equations arising in fluid dynamics. My research focuses on relativistic fluids and water waves.

I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from Vanderbilt University in May 2025 under the supervision of Marcelo Disconzi. At Missouri my mentor is Samuel Walsh. Before that, I graduated from the University of Minnesota, Twin Cities, in May 2019.
